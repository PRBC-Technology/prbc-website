# Updating the Enlarge total

Open `enlarge/progress.csv` and change the amount and date on the second line:

```csv
amount_aud,updated_at
35000,2026-11-08
```

Enter the total in Australian dollars without a dollar sign or comma, and use YYYY-MM-DD for the date. Save the file, then publish it with the website. The website reads this file; changing it on your computer does not update the live site until it is uploaded or deployed.
