LOVE IN ACTION

For our neighbours.
For our community.

On the first Sunday of February to October, we join Neighbour in Need, part of Logan Connect, for Community Service Sunday, taking us beyond our walls to serve people in our local community.

From helping with household and yard tasks to preparing meals, offering prayer or simply sharing kindness, we put our faith into action together.
