We Love Serving Our Local Community

KINGDOM FOCUSED, BUILDING FAITH, STRENGTHENING FAMILIES

Every 1st Sunday of the month, at PRBC we dedicate this Sunday to step beyond our walls and into our community for ‘Community Service Sunday.’ On these special Sundays, we come together to serve those around us in the community — whether it’s helping with household or yard tasks, preparing meals, offering prayer, or simply sharing kindness wherever it’s needed most. We believe that serving others is a meaningful way to reflect Jesus’s love, and through these efforts, we aim to make a difference in the lives of our neighbors. Community Service Sunday is our way of living out our faith with hands and hearts ready to help.

THE CHURCH​

We are a church that believes in Jesus & loves God and people

Our mission is to share the hope of the gospel with those around us and to grow together in faith as we seek to honor God in every area of our lives. Whether you are visiting us for the first time or considering making PRBC your church home, we want you to know that you are warmly welcomed and that there is a place for you here.

As we continue to grow God’s kingdom together, we invite you to join us in worship, fellowship, and service. We believe that God has a unique purpose for each of us, and as we come together in Christ, we are strengthened to live out His love in our families, communities, and beyond. I look forward to meeting you soon and walking alongside you as we strive to grow deeper in our faith and share God’s love with the world around us.
